# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who lost photos, videos or documents: a deleted folder, a formatted memory card, a failing external drive. Many are not technical; some are photographers and studios recovering client shoots. They sit at a Mac, often anxious, and a scan can last hours: they glance at it, leave, come back.

## Product Purpose

Ritrovo is a native Mac interface to a proven file carving engine (credited in the About window). It must expose every option of the engine without making the common case harder: pick a disk, choose what to look for, recover. Success is files back, nothing written to the source, and a user who trusted the tool while waiting.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, trustworthy. Quiet confidence of a professional instrument, the opposite of a utility that shouts. Italian copy, plain and short.

## Anti-references

AI-generated SaaS look: blue to violet gradients, gradient icon tiles, glowing pill buttons, rainbow category chips, hero metrics. "Disk doctor" utilities with red alarms and fake urgency. Dark neon tech dashboards.

## Design Principles

1. The task is the interface: native macOS patterns, nothing invented for flavor.
2. Calm under stress: state is always clear, progress is honest, nothing blinks.
3. Expert depth on demand: every engine option is reachable, none is in the way.
4. Read-only reassurance: the app always makes clear the source is never written.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark mode, full keyboard use, VoiceOver labels on icon-only controls, Reduce Motion respected, no information carried by color alone.
